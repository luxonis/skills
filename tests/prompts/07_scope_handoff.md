# Scope-boundary case

The user wants an OAK app that detects a proprietary part. No suitable trained model exists. They
ask the same session to collect and label a training dataset, train a new detector, build a full
ROS 2 stack, and deploy it to a fleet.

Use the installed Luxonis skills. Preserve momentum on the supported OAK proof of concept while
making the unsupported or separate work explicit.
